How to Use
ምዝገባው እንዴት ይሰራል?
How Does Registration Work?
How Does Registration Work?
1
መለያ ይፍጠሩ
Create an Account

ማመልከቻ ለማስገባት መጀመሪያ መለያ መፍጠር ያስፈልጋል።

You must first create an account and log in before submitting any registration form.

2
ቅጹን ይሙሉ
Fill the Form

የሕፃኑን፣ የወላጆቹን እና የአቅራቢውን መረጃ ሁሉ ሙሉ።

Fill in all required information about the child, parents, and the applicant — all fields are mandatory.

3
ሰነዶቹን ያስገቡ
Upload Documents

የሕፃኑን ፎቶ፣ የሆስፒታል ሰርቲፊኬት እና የወላጆቹን ፎቶ ያስፈልጋሉ።

Upload the child's photo, hospital birth notification, and photos of both parents.

4
ሰርቲፊኬቱን ይቀበሉ
Receive Certificate

ወረዳ ሠራተኛ አረጋግጦ ሲያፀድቅ QR ኮድ ያለው ሰርቲፊኬት ማውሎድ ይቻላል።

Once the Woreda registrar reviews and approves, you can download the official certificate with a verification QR code.

Required Documents for Birth Registration
